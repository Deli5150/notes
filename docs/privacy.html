Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Your notes stay yours

Delta Notes (“Notes” once installed) stores your notes, folders, attachments and settings on your own device. There are no Notes servers and no Notes accounts, and nothing is collected, sold or shared. Locked notes are encrypted with your password, which never leaves your device.

Image Wand (optional)

If you add your own Gemini API key (Settings → Text → Image Wand), making a picture sends what you describe to Google’s Gemini API, along with the note’s text if you tick “Use this note for ideas”, or the drawing when you turn a canvas or sketch into a picture. Nothing is sent otherwise. The key stays encrypted on your device and is never synced. Google’s own terms and privacy policy apply to those requests.

Connected Services (optional)

If you connect GitHub, Todoist, Linear, Jira or Google Tasks in Settings → Connected Services, Notes reads your items there (issues, pull requests, tasks) to show them as notes, using the token or sign-in you give it. It only reads, never changes anything at those services. Tokens stay encrypted on the device where you entered them and are never synced; the notes made from those items sync like any other notes if you use Sync with Google. Each service’s own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Sync with Google (optional)

If you turn on Sync with Google, Notes asks for access to its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission) and your email address (to show which account is syncing). Notes keeps your notes and their attachments there so your devices stay the same. It cannot see or touch any other files in your Drive. Turn Sync off at any time to sign out; you can delete Notes’ data by removing Notes under Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Notes’ use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other connections

On a Mac with Apple Intelligence, Generate title uses Apple’s on-device model, so the note never leaves your Mac.

If you add your own Anthropic API key (Settings → Text) and choose Claude for titles, pressing Generate title sends that note’s text to Anthropic’s API to suggest a title; nothing is sent otherwise, and the key stays encrypted on your computer. Anthropic’s own privacy policy applies to those requests.

When you paste a web address, Notes fetches that page to show a link preview. Importing from Apple Notes reads the Notes app on your Mac, on your Mac only. Update checks go to GitHub. Feedback opens GitHub in your browser, where you choose what to send.
