# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-21

The first release of Notes, for Mac and Windows.

- Folders (nested), Smart Folders, #tags, pinned notes, Quick Notes and Recently Deleted (30 days)
- Title, Heading, Subheading, Body and Monostyled text; bulleted, dashed and numbered lists; checklists; block quotes; tables
- Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, superscript and subscript; highlights in Apple’s five colours; text colours; fonts (including the ones on your computer), sizes, alignment and indentation
- Pictures, scanned documents, sketches, audio recordings, videos, PDFs, any file, and link previews
- Math Notes: type an expression and = for the answer, with variables
- Collapsible sections, Lines & Grids, links between notes (type >>), find & replace
- Locked notes with one password, and Touch ID on Macs
- List and Gallery views, sorting and date groups, search, the Attachments Browser
- Android app (early version): the same Notes on your phone, with a phone layout (the list, then the note full screen) and the back button, Sync with Google, and in-app updates (downloads the new APK and opens the installer)
- Nothing theme: notes in the sidebar sit on solid cards, so they’re easy to read over the dot grid
- Note previews in the list show the body again for notes with a title
- Settings → Import shows each app’s own icon
- A note closed or left with nothing in it is deleted, so there are never empty notes
- Floating Quick Note: a small note above every app, shown and hidden with ⌃⌘N (Ctrl+Alt+N on Windows)
- Every keyboard shortcut can be changed in Settings → Shortcuts (Ctrl instead of ⌘ on Windows)
- ⌘W (Ctrl+W) closes the open note, then the window (⇧⌘W closes the window)
- Generate title reads the whole note and sums it up: with Apple Intelligence on the Mac (on-device), or Claude with your own API key
- Nothing and Pixel themes, with their own fonts and shapes
- ⌘T (Ctrl+T) for a new note and ⌘S (Ctrl+S) to show or hide the sidebar, as in Arc
- Sync with Google (your own Drive app folder); notes changed on two devices at once keep both versions
- Import from Apple Notes (straight from the Notes app on a Mac), Samsung Notes, Notion, Evernote, Google Keep, Bear, Simplenote, Standard Notes, Obsidian/Markdown, Word, PDF, text and HTML
- Export to PDF, Markdown, HTML and plain text; print; export everything as Markdown; full backups
- Twelve colour themes, each in light and dark, and a see-through sidebar
- In-app updates and feedback
